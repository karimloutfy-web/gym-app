Rep Star terms of use

Effective 11 November 2026 · Contact: support@rep-star.app

These terms apply when you use Rep Star. Most of the app works without an account. When you sign in, you also agree to the community rules below, because your finished workouts, name and profile picture are shown to other people in the Feed.

Community rules: zero tolerance

Rep Star has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You may not post, or use as your name or picture, anything that:

  • harasses, bullies, threatens or impersonates anyone;
  • is hateful or demeaning about race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other group;
  • is sexual, pornographic, violent or gory;
  • promotes self-harm, eating disorders, illegal drugs or other illegal activity;
  • is spam, advertising or a scam;
  • shares anyone's private information without their permission.

How we enforce them

  • Offensive words in names and in what you post are filtered out automatically.
  • Anyone can report a post or a person from the ⋯ menu in the Feed, and block them so they disappear from their feed.
  • We review every report within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and remove the account of anyone who posted it.

Your account and content

You need to be at least 13 to sign in. Keep your Apple or Google sign-in secure; you're responsible for what's posted from your account. You keep ownership of what you post. You let us store it and show it to other Rep Star users so the Feed works. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time in Settings → Feed → Delete account.

Not medical advice

Programs, weights, calorie targets and body-fat numbers are general fitness estimates, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training or diet plan, and stop if something hurts.

The service

Rep Star is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes. We may change or end features, and we may suspend accounts that break these terms. Your data is handled as described in the privacy policy.

Changes

If these terms change, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the new terms apply.
