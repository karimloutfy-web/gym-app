Rep Star privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Contact: support@rep-star.app

Rep Star is a training log. It works without an account: your program, workouts, body measurements and food log are stored only on your device. If you sign in to use the Feed, the workouts you finish are shared with other Rep Star users. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your program, workout history, body entries (weight, body fat, measurements, skinfolds), nutrition settings and food log are kept in the app's storage on your phone. They never leave the device unless you export a backup yourself. Settings → Your data → Erase everything removes them.

Online data is stored with Supabase, our database provider.

Services the app talks to

  • Food search: what you type in food search is sent to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to find matching foods. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Sign-in: Apple or Google, only when you choose to sign in.
  • Fonts and app code: loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which see your IP address like any website does.

Health information

Calorie, body-fat and training numbers are general fitness estimates, not medical advice. Body data stays on your device and is never posted to the Feed.

Deleting your data

  • Online account: Settings → Feed → Delete account permanently deletes your profile, picture, posts, follows, kudos, blocks and reports.
  • On your device: Settings → Your data → Erase everything, or delete the app.

Children

Rep Star isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and note the change in the app.
