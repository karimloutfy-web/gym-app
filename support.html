Common questions

How do I get my workouts onto a new phone?
Sign in with the same Apple or Google account in Settings → Feed → Sign in. Your program, history, body and food logs come back from your backup.

Apple Health data isn't showing.
Open the Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Rep Star and turn the categories on. Health data stays on your phone.

The rest timer isn't on my lock screen.
Open the Settings app → Rep Star and allow Live Activities and notifications.

Someone in the Feed is being abusive.
Tap ⋯ on their post → Report, then Block. We review reports within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Feed → Delete account. This removes your profile, posts and backup from our servers.
